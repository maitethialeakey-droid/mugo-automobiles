# Kenya-Relevant Catalogue Coverage

The expanded dashboard catalogue will use **draft-only inventory templates** rather than published vehicle offers. Every imported record will require stock, VIN, pricing, condition, media, and compliance verification before it can be published to the public storefront.

The initial coverage prioritizes Toyota, Nissan, Honda, Mitsubishi, Mazda, Subaru, Suzuki, Daihatsu, Isuzu, Ford, Volkswagen, Mercedes-Benz, BMW, Audi, Land Rover, Lexus, Hyundai, Kia, Peugeot, and Volvo. Kenya-focused used-vehicle ranking data highlights models including the Toyota Land Cruiser Prado, RAV4, Harrier, Corolla Fielder, Mazda CX-5 and CX-8, Honda Vezel, Nissan X-Trail, and Subaru Forester. [TCV Kenya ranking](https://www.tc-v.com/popularranking/?c=404) also lists categories spanning sedans, hatchbacks, SUVs, wagons, vans, trucks, and buses.

For commercial coverage, current market reporting identifies Isuzu, Toyota, Tata, Mitsubishi, and Mahindra among top brands, with Isuzu D-Max, Toyota Hilux, and Isuzu MU-X among cited models. [Focus2move Kenya vehicle sales](https://www.focus2move.com/kenyan-vehicles-sales-2026/) provides the contextual reference. These sources guide catalogue breadth only; they do not validate real inventory availability.
